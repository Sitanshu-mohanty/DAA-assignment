# Test Cases

## Test Case 1 - Pair Exists

Input:
```text
5
1 4 7 10 15
2 5 8 11 20
12
```

Expected:
```text
Pair found: 4 + 8 = 12
```

## Test Case 2 - No Pair

Input:
```text
4
1 3 5 7
2 4 6 8
20
```

Expected:
```text
No pair found.
```

## Test Case 3 - Duplicate Values

Input:
```text
5
1 2 2 6 9
3 4 5 7 10
7
```

Expected:
```text
Pair found: 2 + 5 = 7
```

## Test Case 4 - Negative Values

Input:
```text
4
-10 -4 3 8
-2 1 5 9
-3
```

Expected:
```text
Pair found: -4 + 1 = -3
```

## Test Case 5 - Single Element

Input:
```text
1
10
5
15
```

Expected:
```text
Pair found: 10 + 5 = 15
```
